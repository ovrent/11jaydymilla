## 7. Professional Identity

### Who is JayDyMilla?
JayDyMilla is the artistic moniker of **Jay Miller**, an Asheville, North Carolina-based songwriter, 5-string banjo player, and musician whose work synthesizes **folk storytelling, Appalachian acoustic instrumentation, and modern rhythmic production (hip-hop beats, electric guitar, and bass lines)**.

### The Name & Symbolism
- **Pronunciation**: Jay “Di” (diamond 💎) or “D” (dragon 🐉) Millah.
- **Bio Glyphs**: `💎🐉🌋`
  - `💎` = Diamond clarity, self-realization, inner radiance.
  - `🐉` = Dragon, primal creative fire, kundalini/breath energy ("Sometimes the dragon sings through breath and rhythm").
  - `🌋` = Volcano, seismic personal transformation, eruption of bottled creativity.

### Core Musical Niche: "Banjo Folk-Hop / Conscious Shamanic Folk"
Unlike traditional bluegrass or orthodox country, JayDyMilla uses the banjo as a contemporary rhythmic engine. He pairs banjo rolls and clawhammer patterns with urban grooves, electronic synthesizers, punchy 808-style bass, and introspective, soul-searching vocal narratives.

### Creative Philosophy
Rooted in a 15-year hiatus devoted to fatherhood and deep meditation, followed by intense study of shamanic power songs (17 daily practice songs created with buffalo drums and rattles), Jay’s music focuses on:
- Soul retrieval & confronting inner shadow ("Soul Creature")
- Radical authenticity and personal transformation ("Fire Horse", "Cool&YkIt")
- Nostalgia, vulnerability, and love ("By The Window", "If You Miss Me")
- Joyful creative freedom and living without pretense ("Neon Limelight", "Lost It")
