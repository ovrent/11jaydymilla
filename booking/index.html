Technical Rider & Stage Setup

JayDyMilla operates with a streamlined, low-friction production footprint designed for rapid sound checks and pristine acoustic reproduction.

  • Banjo: 1x Balanced XLR / D.I. Channel with 48V Phantom
  • Vocal: 1x Shure SM58 or equivalent dynamic microphone
  • Backing Track / Rhythm: 2x Balanced Stereo 1/4" D.I. line-in
  • Monitoring: 1x Wedge monitor or Stereo IEM feed
